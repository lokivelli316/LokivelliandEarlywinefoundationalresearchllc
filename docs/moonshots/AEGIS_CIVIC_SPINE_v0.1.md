# AEGIS Civic Spine v0.1

Status: SYSTEMS-ENGINEERING SEED / NO DEPLOYMENT CLAIM

AEGIS Civic Spine is the integration architecture for the existing Smart Roads, physical Project Aegis, DTDFM, and Aegis Moat Roads concept family.

The design target is a modular civil service backplane: infrastructure that can carry and expose upgradeable communications, power/data conduit, sensing, drainage/storm-management interfaces, inspection points, and replaceable service modules without repeatedly treating every utility as an isolated buried asset.

## v0.1 rule

Old performance, cost, payback, "stormproof", self-healing, graphene, lifespan, and valuation claims are inputs to re-test, not inherited facts.

## First engineering gate

Digital twin before physical scale.

`schemas/aegis-civic-spine-twin.schema.json` records:
- corridor sections;
- modules and interfaces;
- performance claims and their evidence state;
- inspections and faults;
- FMEA entries;
- repairs and supersession.

## One-meter demonstrator objective

The first physical demonstrator should falsify interface and maintainability assumptions, not mimic a mile of finished road.

Measurable categories:
- access/replacement time;
- water ingress detection;
- conduit segregation;
- module removal/reinstallation;
- inspection visibility;
- fault localization;
- documentation/replay of repair history.

Qualified structural, electrical, telecom, roadway, and regulatory review remains an external gate before deployment claims.
