02 — Method

Adversarial audit, by design

Nothing is released because it sounds right. Work is routed through a panel of separate AI systems whose disagreement is the point — each seat catches what the others miss, and no single voice is allowed to dominate. This is IChORDME.

Seven models, seven seats at the table. Each letter is one system in the audit panel. Agreement between them only counts when it survives the ones built to disagree.
