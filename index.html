About

Lokivelli & Earlywine Foundational Research LLC is an independent research organization focused on systems architecture, theoretical physics, and publishing.

Our work is built through adversarial audit — we test our systems rigorously and publish our findings, including the failures, to advance collective understanding.

Methodology

We believe in transparency through adversarial testing. Every system we build is subjected to rigorous audit, and we publish our findings — including failures and limitations — to help others learn from our experience.
